# Challenges

You now know the building blocks behind nearly every program: **events, variables, if/else, loops and functions**. Try one of these mini projects before moving on to radio. Each one uses several of those ideas together.

### Rock, Paper, Scissors
Shake to pick randomly between rock, paper and scissors, and show a picture for each. Play against the person next to you.
Tutorial: [makecode.microbit.org/projects/rock-paper-scissors](https://makecode.microbit.org/projects/rock-paper-scissors)

*Extra:* add a secret special move that only appears 1 time in 10.

### Reaction timer
Wait a random amount of time, then light up the screen. Press **A** as fast as you can. Show how many milliseconds you took. (Hint: **running time (ms)** is in **Input → more**.)

### Hot Potato
Pass the micro:bit around. A hidden timer counts down and whoever is holding it when it runs out loses!
Tutorial: [makecode.microbit.org/projects/hot-potato](https://makecode.microbit.org/projects/hot-potato)

### Make your own
Anything you like, as simple or as complex as you want. Have a look at [makecode.microbit.org/projects](https://makecode.microbit.org/projects) for ideas.
