# Make Your Own Power-ups

Now make the power-ups your own. Remember to **download and test after every change**.

### Level 1: Tweak it
- Change how **long** each power-up lasts (the **pause** inside each function)
- Change the **speeds**. Is a boost of 100 too much on your track?
- Change the **icons**, or draw your own with **show leds**

### Level 2: Add effects
- Add a sound to **speedUp** and **slowDown** (V2 only, **Music** section)
- Make the boost **flash** the screen using a **repeat** loop instead of showing one icon
- Make the banana spin go the **other** way

### Level 3: Invent a new power-up
Change what one pair of cards does by writing a new **function** and calling it instead. Ideas:
- **Reverse controls:** for 5 seconds, left becomes right and forward becomes backwards. *(Hint: you'll need another flag variable, and the radio event will need to check it.)*
- **Wiggle:** zig-zag left and right a few times
- **Shield:** a card that makes you immune to the next banana

### Level 4 (advanced): Register a new card
Every card has a different ID, so a new card needs its number added to the code:
1. Add an **on button A pressed** event that does **show number** with **Try read ID** (from the **MFRC522** section)
2. Hold your new card against the reader and press **A**. Write down the long number that scrolls past. (If it shows -1, the card wasn't detected. Hold it closer and try again.)
3. Make a new variable, e.g. `card7`, set it to that number, and add it to an **if** in the forever loop

<br>

> **Race day tip:** before the race, label your car **and** your remote with your team's radio group number. Then nobody can mix them up!
