# The Car (Receiver)

Setting up the car

The **car is the receiver micro:bit**. While your teammate codes the remote, you'll code the car to listen for messages and drive the motors.

The car is a **Kitronik :MOVE Motor** buggy. The micro:bit slots into the front and controls the two wheels: one motor on the left, one on the right.

### Step 1
Create a new project, e.g. **RC-Car**

![step1](img/step1.png)

### Step 2
Click **Extensions** at the bottom of the toolbox.

![step2](img/step2.png)

### Step 3
Search **"kitronik move motor"** and click on it. A new **MOVE Motor** section will appear in your toolbox.

![step3](img/step3.png)

## How does a car turn with only two motors?

There's no steering wheel! The car turns by running its wheels at **different speeds or directions**:

| Left wheel | Right wheel | Car does |
|------------|-------------|----------|
| Forward | Forward | Drives forward |
| Reverse | Reverse | Reverses |
| Slow / stopped | Forward | Turns left |
| Forward | Slow / stopped | Turns right |
| Forward | Reverse | Spins on the spot! |

The **move [Forward] at speed [80]** block handles this for you. Pick Forward, Reverse, Left or Right from the dropdown.
