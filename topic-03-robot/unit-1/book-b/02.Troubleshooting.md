# Troubleshooting

Car not doing what you expect? Work through this list. Real engineers call this **debugging**.

| Problem | Things to check |
|---------|-----------------|
| Car doesn't move at all | Is the car switched on? Are the batteries charged? Did the code actually download (wait for the second flash)? |
| Car doesn't respond to the remote | Do the remote and car have the **same radio group**? Is the remote switched on (can you see its icon)? |
| Car responds to someone else's remote | Two teams have the same radio group. Pick a new, unique number for **both** your remote and car. |
| Car drives off on its own | Your remote isn't sending `"stop"` when the stick is centred. Check the at-rest ranges with **show number**. |
| Car goes the wrong direction | Check the letters match on both sides (`"F"` vs `"f"`). Check your X/Y numbers on the remote. |
| One direction never works | Remember **else if** checks top to bottom. Is an earlier condition always true and blocking it? |
| Car is too fast to control | Lower the speeds in the car code. |

> **Debugging tip:** add a **show string receivedString** block at the top of your car's radio received event. Now the car shows every message it gets, so you can see exactly what the remote is sending. Remove it once you've found the problem, because scrolling text slows the car's reactions down.
