# The Remote (Sender)

Setting up the joystick

The **remote is the sender micro:bit**. One person in your team codes the remote while the other codes the car (next lab). You'll test them together at the end.

Your remote is a micro:bit plugged into a **Joystick:bit** controller. It has an analog stick (the "rocker"), four coloured buttons, and the micro:bit's own **A** and **B** buttons on top.

![controller](img/controller.png)

### Step 1
Create a new project, e.g. **RC-Car-Remote**

![Step 1](img/step1.png)

### Step 2
The joystick blocks aren't built in, so you need to add them as an **extension**. Click **Extensions** at the bottom of the toolbox.

![Step 2](img/step2.png)

### Step 3
Search **"joystickbit"** and click on it. A new **Joystickbit** section will appear in your toolbox.

![Step 3](img/step3.png)
